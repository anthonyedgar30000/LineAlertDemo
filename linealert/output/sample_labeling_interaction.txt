LineAlert Labeling Guide Interaction
====================================
User reports: "Label alignment is off"

Matched Symptom: Label Alignment is Off

Relevant Checks:
- Peel tip square to bottle?
- Is bottle stable during application?
- Are guide rings centered?
- Is issue constant or intermittent?
- Does issue worsen at higher speed?

Recommended Actions:
- Adjust Peel Angle
- Adjust Hold Down
- Check Guide Rings
- Increase Label Delay
- Reduce Speed Temporarily

Related Symptoms if Problem Persists:
- Bubbles on Labels: Insufficient pressure time or unstable contact can create both bubbles and alignment errors.

Escalation Guidance:
- Issue continues after all adjustments.
- Multiple issues are occurring at the same time.
- Mechanical damage or worn parts are observed.
- Unusual noises or air leaks are present.
- Need replacement parts.

Key Adjustment Areas:
- Peel Angle: Peel plate angle setting
- Hold Down: Hold-down pressure or position
- Guide Rings: Product guidance
- Label Delay: Timing of label application

Key Point: Alignment problems are usually caused by geometry, stability, or timing offset.
